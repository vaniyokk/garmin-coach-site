Personal Coach

A private training-planning tool that reads the account owner's Garmin training and recovery data, plans strength, mobility and swim sessions around it, and sends those sessions to their Garmin watch as structured workouts.

What it does

  • Reads activity, sleep, HRV, heart-rate, stress, Body Battery and step data from the user's own Garmin Connect account, with their explicit authorization.
  • Summarizes recovery and training load, and adjusts the week's plan to it.
  • Builds structured workouts from Garmin's exercise library (sets, reps, weights, rest, form cues) and schedules them on the user's Garmin Connect calendar, so they appear on the watch.

Who uses it

Personal Coach is an internal tool of FOP Kuzmin Ivan Oleksandrovych, a Ukrainian sole proprietorship, used by its owner for his own training. It is not sold, offered to the public, or used to provide services to third parties.

How data is handled

Health and activity data stays on devices the business controls and is processed by Anthropic's Claude to produce analysis and training plans. Nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or passed to other parties. The user can revoke access at any time in Garmin Connect. Full details are in the privacy policy.
