Privacy policy

Effective 26 October 2026

Who is responsible

FOP Kuzmin Ivan Oleksandrovych (ФОП Кузьмін Іван Олександрович), a sole proprietorship registered in Ukraine ("we"), operates Garmin Coach and is the controller of the data it processes. Contact: ivankuzmin.dev@gmail.com.

Who this applies to

Garmin Coach is an internal tool, used only by the business owner with his own Garmin account. We do not offer it to the public and do not collect data from anyone who has not connected their own account to it.

What we collect

DataSource
Activity summaries and activity files (including strength sets, heart rate, duration, training effect and load)Garmin Connect, with the user's authorization
Daily health data: sleep, heart-rate variability, resting heart rate, stress, Body Battery, respiration, steps, body weightGarmin Connect, with the user's authorization
Training plans, workouts and training notes the user writes, including symptoms they choose to logThe user

Activity files can include GPS tracks of the user's own workouts; they are used only for the purpose below. We do not collect data about other people.

Why we use it

Only to analyze the user's training and recovery, plan their training, and send structured workouts back to their own Garmin account and calendar.

Where it is processed and who receives it

  • Our own devices. Garmin data is stored locally on devices we control, not in a public or shared database.
  • Anthropic (Claude). Data is processed by Anthropic's Claude models to produce analysis and training plans, under Anthropic's commercial terms. This may happen in processing environments operated by Anthropic.
  • Garmin. Workouts we create are sent to the user's own Garmin Connect account.
  • Google Calendar. Session titles, times and exercise descriptions are added to the user's own calendar. Health data is not sent there.
  • GitHub. Our source code and training plans are kept in a private repository. Raw Garmin health data is not stored there.

We do not sell data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone else.

How long we keep it

As long as the user keeps their account connected. A user can revoke access at any time in Garmin Connect (Settings → Connected Apps) and ask us to delete their data; we delete it within 30 days of the request.

Security

Access credentials are stored with permissions restricted to the device owner, are never committed to source control, and are used only to call Garmin's APIs on the user's behalf.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and its effective date.
