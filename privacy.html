Privacy policy

Effective 26 October 2026 · updated 26 October 2026

Who is responsible

FOP Kuzmin Ivan Oleksandrovych (ФОП Кузьмін Іван Олександрович), a sole proprietorship registered in Ukraine ("we"), operates Personal Coach and is the controller of the data it processes. Contact: ivankuzmin.dev@gmail.com.

Who this applies to

Personal Coach is an internal tool, used only by the business owner with his own Garmin account. We do not offer it to the public and do not collect data from anyone who has not connected their own account to it.

What we collect

DataSource
Activity summaries and activity files (including strength sets, heart rate, duration, training effect and load)Garmin Connect, with the user's authorization
Daily health data: sleep, heart-rate variability, resting heart rate, stress, Body Battery, respiration, steps, body weightGarmin Connect, with the user's authorization
Training plans, workouts, a training and health journal, and the health background the user chooses to record (for example symptoms, past illnesses and test results) The user

Activity files can include GPS tracks of the user's own workouts; they are used only for the purpose below. We do not collect data about other people.

Why we use it

Only to analyze the user's training, recovery and health, plan their training, give them health guidance based on their own data, and send structured workouts back to their own Garmin account and calendar.

Where it is processed and who receives it

  • Cloudflare (hosting and storage). Our backend runs on Cloudflare in an account we control. It holds the Garmin authorization tokens and stores the Garmin data together with the user's plans, workouts and notes. It is not a public or shared database. Older copies of the data may also remain on devices we control.
  • Anthropic (Claude). The user works with Personal Coach through Anthropic's Claude. Data is processed by Anthropic's Claude models to produce analysis, guidance and training plans, under Anthropic's commercial terms. This may happen in processing environments operated by Anthropic.
  • Garmin. Workouts we create are sent to the user's own Garmin Connect account.
  • Google Calendar. Session titles, times and exercise descriptions are added to the user's own calendar. Health data is not sent there.
  • GitHub. Our source code and some older training notes are kept in a private repository. Raw Garmin data is not stored there.

We do not sell data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone else.

AI transparency statement

Personal Coach uses an AI system, Anthropic's Claude, to read the user's Garmin data and write training analysis, health guidance, workout plans and the workouts sent back to Garmin. The AI gives general health information and suggestions based on the user's data; it does not diagnose, and urgent symptoms are referred to medical care at once.

  • Garmin data and anything derived from it, including our prompts, conversations and the AI's outputs, are never used to train, evaluate, benchmark or improve any AI model, and we have opted out of model training on our Anthropic account.
  • No AI model, dataset or knowledge base is built from Garmin data.
  • Consent. Before a Garmin account is connected, the user is shown this statement and must explicitly agree to AI processing; the time of consent is recorded.
  • Withdrawing consent. The user can withdraw at any time by disconnecting Personal Coach in Garmin Connect (Settings → Connected Apps) or by emailing us. AI processing stops immediately and the data is deleted as described below.

Your rights

The user can ask us to access, export, correct or delete their data, or to stop processing it, by emailing ivankuzmin.dev@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days. Exports are provided as JSON and the original activity files.

How long we keep it

As long as the user keeps their account connected. A user can revoke access at any time in Garmin Connect (Settings → Connected Apps); when Garmin tells us access was revoked, our backend deletes the authorization tokens at once. On request we delete the stored data, on Cloudflare and on any of our devices, within 30 days.

Security

Access credentials are stored with permissions restricted to the device owner or as encrypted Cloudflare secrets, are never committed to source control, and are used only to call Garmin's APIs on the user's behalf. Every function of our backend requires authentication.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and its effective date.
